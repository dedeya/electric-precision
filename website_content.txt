SCADA, Automation & Energy Management Solutions

About Electric Precision Labs

Electric Precision Labs is a technology solutions company specialising
in SCADA systems, substation automation, energy management, and
operational technology (OT) cybersecurity for critical infrastructure
and operational environments. We design and deliver reliable monitoring,
control, and secure system architectures that support industry-standard
protocols and integrate with both legacy and modern infrastructure.

Our Services

SCADA & Control Systems

We deliver SCADA and control system solutions that provide real-time
visibility and secure operational control of critical assets, including
centralised and remote monitoring, data acquisition, and
operator-friendly Human Machine Interfaces (HMIs).

Substation Automation Solutions

Our substation automation solutions support transmission and
distribution networks using Remote Terminal Units (RTUs) and HMIs to
enable efficient monitoring and control.

Industry-Standard Protocol Support

Our systems support IEC 61850, DNP3, IEC 101/104, and Modbus protocols
to ensure interoperability across multi-vendor environments and existing
infrastructure.

Energy Management System (EMS) Solutions

We provide local, remote, and cloud-based energy management solutions
including revenue metering, power quality metering, and statistical
metering to support operational and commercial requirements.

OT Cybersecurity Architecture Design & Vulnerability Assessment

Electric Precision Labs provides OT cybersecurity services focused on
protecting critical control systems and operational technology
environments. Our services include secure OT network architecture design
and vulnerability assessments aligned with industrial and utility
environments. We help organisations identify security risks, improve
system resilience, and support compliance with cybersecurity best
practices for SCADA, substations, and energy systems.

Building Management Systems (BMS)

As an extension of our automation expertise, we offer Building
Management System solutions to improve monitoring, control, and
operational efficiency of building services.
