Electric Precision Labs Logo Variants

Files:
- logo-epl.svg: Primary app icon/logo mark for UI and favicon use.
- logo-epl-wordmark.svg: Horizontal lockup for headers, decks, and social banners.
- logo-epl-mono.svg: Monochrome variant for print/emboss/low-ink use.

Quick guidance:
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the width of one hex node.
- Avoid stretching logos; scale proportionally.
- Use the monochrome variant when color contrast is insufficient.
